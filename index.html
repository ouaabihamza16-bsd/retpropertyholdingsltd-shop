About us

A Barnsley property holding company

RET Property Holdings Ltd was incorporated in England and Wales on 19 June 2024 and is based in Barnsley, South Yorkshire. The company is run by its director, Christopher James Wright.

As a holding company, we concentrate on our own property: buying it, looking after it, letting it and, where appropriate, selling it.
